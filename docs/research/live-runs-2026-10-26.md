# Live model runs, 2026-10-26 (sample metrics)

`scripts/try-live-plans.ts`: LIVE NVIDIA models, MOCK metrics (`apps/worker/test/fixtures.ts`) until the
Zetaris adapter (lane B) is merged.

| Run | Router (Nemotron 3.5 Lightning, thinking off) | Plans (Nemotron 3 Ultra 550B) | Result |
|---|---|---|---|
| Router with thinking on, 400 then 2,048 max tokens | Cut off ("length") | — | Lightning reasons for ~1,400+ tokens before answering; the client correctly rejected the incomplete answer |
| Probe: `chat_template_kwargs.enable_thinking=false` | 0.6 s, 41 tokens, valid | — | Adopted for the front door |
| Ultra thinking off, first prompt | 0.8 s, valid | Rejected twice (projections over the size limit) | Limits added to the prompt |
| Ultra thinking off, + limits | 1.5 s, valid | Rejected twice (factor cap of 10 too tight for count × value) | Factor range widened to ±1,000 |
| Ultra thinking off, + factors | 0.6 s, valid | Rejected twice: proposed prices and percentages written as if they were facts | **Levers** added (declared proposed settings) |
| Ultra thinking off, + levers | 0.5 s, valid | Rejected twice: 6 undeclared numbers | Thinking off is too careless for plans |
| **Ultra thinking ON** | 0.7 s, valid (finance, risk: money, 0.95) | **Passed on attempt 1, 81.6 s, 5,467 tokens** | Default: `PLAN_THINKING=on` |

What the validator proves:
- every number in a plan traces to a Zetaris metric, to a projection whose arithmetic it recomputes, or to
  a lever the plan declares as a proposed choice;
- salon and online numbers aren't blended without a labeled combined metric.

What it doesn't prove: that an assumption (e.g. "a 20% price rise lifts revenue 40%") is wise. That
judgment stays with the people who approve the plan.
